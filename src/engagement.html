Engagement

How we engage.

Three ways to work together — discrete project work, a complete launch package, and ongoing retainers — each scoped to your raise, your timeline, and the standard the capital markets expect.

Model 01

Project work.

Pick exactly the deliverables you need — investor maps, decks, websites, fact sheets, branding, or GIS films. Each piece is scoped on its own terms and delivered against a single fixed quote, so you know what you’re committing to before we begin.

Best for companies addressing a specific gap — a deck ahead of a roadshow, a refreshed map set for a financing, a fact sheet before a conference — without committing to a broader programme.

Model 02

The Launch Package.

The full investor-relations foundation in one engagement — everything an exploration company needs to become investable, delivered together as a single fixed quote at a meaningful saving versus commissioning each piece on its own.

What’s included
  • A complete investor map set
  • A polished investor deck
  • A purpose-built website
  • A clean, current fact sheet
  • A coherent brand identity

Scoped as one engagement so the maps, deck, site, fact sheet, and brand speak with a single voice — and priced as one fixed quote, at a saving over piecing it together deliverable by deliverable.

Process

How an engagement works.

1
Discovery call
We learn about your deposit, your raise, and your timeline — and where the gaps are in how the market sees you.
2
Scope & fixed quote
We define the deliverables and return a clear, fixed quote — no open-ended estimates, no surprises.
3
You approve
You review the scope and quote, ask questions, and give us the go-ahead before any work begins.
4
We build
We produce the work to an institutional standard, with checkpoints so you stay informed throughout.
5
Ongoing support
As your story advances, we keep your materials current — on a project basis or under a retainer.
