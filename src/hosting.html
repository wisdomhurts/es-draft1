The moment news breaks

A drill result crosses the wire.

It is the most consequential minute in an exploration company’s quarter. A high-grade intercept hits the newswire, and within seconds the people who matter — analysts, fund managers, retail holders — are typing your ticker and landing on your site at the same instant.

For most explorers, that minute is where the cracks show. The site slows or buckles under the spike. The share price on the homepage is yesterday’s. Someone scrambles to paste the release into the newsroom. The email to the investor list goes out hours later, or the next morning. The biggest moment becomes a scramble across four vendors.

On our stack, that same minute runs itself.
